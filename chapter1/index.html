My favorite beverage is Matcha. Matcha contains green tea leaves that have been condensed into a fine powder, which originates in Japan. Their technique made Matcha famous around the world, and can be bought at asian,bulk, or niche supermarkets.I like this drink because with the right brand, it has a dark, earthy, bitter flavour that can be lightened with milk, cream or sugar. It can be drunk hot or cold. I like to put oat-milk or lactose free milk in my Matcha lattes. When I have the powder at home, I drink this in the morning, early when I wake up.I would recommend this flavour to others, especially those who are open minded and want to try new things. Some might not like the 'grass' earthy flavour, but I do.
